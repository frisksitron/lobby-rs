# Settings and appearance

Settings is one page over whatever the Member is looking at, for this computer only: a back arrow, then Voice, Window and Updates sections. Every control has one plain line that says what it does, with no rule between rows: groups stand apart by space, rows inside a group by a smaller gap. Voice has three groups, each under a small muted heading. **Microphone**: the select `input-device` at the column's full width; under it one line with the live level meter `mic-level` (lavender segments, coral near clipping) and the toggle button "Hear yourself" `monitor` (a normal button at rest, lavender while on, and its accessible state says it is pressed: never saved, off at every start and once Settings closes or follows another Lobby; in a call the call plays it, and with none on the page plays the microphone through a call's chain to the speakers itself); under that a one-line slot that is always there (nothing moves) and holds `mic-failure` if the microphone cannot be listened to, else `mic-hint` (too quiet, too loud, clipping), else, while hearing yourself, "Use headphones to avoid feedback." on `monitor-headphones`; then "Mic volume, for every app" with the slider `input-level` and its percent (the chosen microphone's Windows level, which changes it for every app; while Windows has not said what it is the slider is set aside, and when it cannot be changed the reason takes its place). **Speakers**: `output-device`. **Sound**: three rows, no modes or presets: "Cancel echo from speakers" (`echo-cancellation`), "Reduce background noise" (the select `noise-suppression`: Off, Gentle or Strong; Off is `noise_suppression/on` false, Gentle is on with the WebRTC suppressor, Strong on with RNNoise, and the stored values stay `rnnoise`/`webrtc`) and "Even out loud and quiet speech" (the select `compressor`: Off, Gentle or Strong; Off is `compressor/on` false, Gentle is on at amount 50, Strong on at 80, and a stored amount below 65 reads as Gentle). Open a select with a click, then `down` and `enter` pick the next line, as for the devices. A select is 36 px high, like every control of the page (a Windows device select, "Hear yourself", "Check now", the Size stepper's − and + and the Back arrow); the onboarding screens' field and button are 44. Choosing a line changes no row's height. All three rows are always live. With all three off (echo switch off, both selects Off) the voice goes out untouched, the limiter skipped too, with the least delay (the low-delay Opus profile): that is what Studio Mode was, and no setting stores it. Turning any one back on leaves it. Last, after a group-size gap, "Play join and leave sounds" (`sounds`, the UI chimes). A row puts its controls under its line when they do not fit beside it (the small window at a UI size of about 120 percent and up; at 100 percent they all fit), and the selects and the meter line stay full width. Open a select with a click, then `down` and `enter` pick the next line; `/settings/voice/{input,output}/shown` and `/settings/{input,output}_device` show the choice. A device picked in a call moves the call to it at once, with no rejoin. Click a slider's track to set it, `/settings/voice/gain` is the mic volume's percent, or `"Reading"` or `"Unavailable(..)"` while it is set aside. Under fake media (`LOBBY_FAKE_MEDIA=1` and Layers 3 and 4) Mic volume holds a fake volume that starts at 80 and never touches a real microphone. State is under `/settings/voice/` (`echo_cancellation`, `noise_suppression/{on,engine}`, `compressor/{on,amount}`, `gain`, `monitor`, `listening`, what the page opened the microphone for while no call has it: `"Meter"`, `"Monitor"` or null, never `"Meter"` under fake media). Scroll it fully under the page header before clicking: the header covers its top rows (`settings-body` starts below it). The Window section is "Size" and, where there is a tray, "Keep Lobby running in the tray when the window closes" (`close-to-tray`). Updates (`updates`) is one line that says the state, with its button on the right: "Lobby <version> is up to date.", "Lobby <version>. Checking for updates…", "Lobby <version>. It checks for updates every hour." (not checked yet), "Lobby <version>. Couldn't reach GitHub. It tries again within the hour.", "Downloading Lobby <new>… <pct>%", "Lobby <new> is ready." with `settings-update-restart` (Restart to update), "Lobby <new> couldn't be downloaded.", and in a build without a release feed "Lobby <version>. This build doesn't update itself." Where it applies `update-check` (Check now) asks the feed at once, and `/settings/updates` is the status (`Current(NotYet)`, `Current(Checking)`, `Current(UpToDate)`, `Current(Unreachable)`, `Downloading {..}`, `Ready {..}`, `Failed {..}`). A drive reaches it with `LOBBY_UPDATE_URL` pointing at a feed laid out like GitHub Releases (`<base>/latest/download/latest.json`). It has no tabs and no title, and scrolls in the small window. The Member's own name, picture and email are not here: they are on the Member's own card (a right-click on their own row). A Lobby's icon and Leave Lobby are in that Lobby's menu in the switcher (see `lobbies-and-window.md`). The choices apply at once and are kept in `state.json`. The app is always dark.

## Sub-features

- `open` shows the page with Ctrl+, on every route, or from the cog in the title bar (right of the bell over a Lobby, at the right edge on the URL step and Sign In). The cog shows pressed while the page is open, and pressing it again closes it. Esc and the Back control return to where the Member was too.
- `own-picture` is Change picture… (`picture-change`, the OS file picker, one file; it cannot be limited to images, so the Lobby refuses a non-image or a too big file and the Issue `account.picture_failed` says why) and, while one is set, Remove (`picture-remove`) on the Member's own card. Change closes the card and the picker goes on. The Lobby makes a 256 px square of the middle of the picture. Everyone sees it at once, in the Member list, the Voice strip and on every Message including old ones; initials stand in while it loads. The dump has `/lobby/member_panel/own/picture` (`set`, `busy`), `/lobby/members/<name>/avatar` and `avatar_shown`, and `avatar` and `avatar_shown` on each of `/lobby/chat/rows`. Script the picker with `{"host": {"pick": [["photo-640x480.png"]]}}`.
- `own-name` is the name on the Member's own card. The pencil (`rename-open`) turns the name's own line into a field (`rename-field`) with Save (`rename-save`), and nothing below it moves. Save is disabled while the text is the current name, is not a username (3 to 32 letters, digits, `_` and `-`, with the rule said on the email's line) or a Save is out. A name another Member, a removed Member or a waiting Ask to Join holds, in any case, is "That name is taken." on the email's line (`rename-said`). Enter saves, Esc cancels (a second Esc closes the card), and the typed text survives other clicks in the card. Everyone sees the new name at once, on their Member list and on old Messages.
- `own-email` is the line under the name (`card-email`): the address the Member's Magic Codes go to, read-only, as the Lobby said it in READY. The dump has `/lobby/member_panel/own/email`.
- `size` scales the whole UI from 80 to 150 percent in steps of 10 (Ctrl+=, Ctrl+Plus, Ctrl+- and Ctrl+0 for 100, or the stepper). Layout sizes are rems, so one font size on the theme scales everything; the window's width and height are compared with the layout rules in design pixels (width over scale), so a bigger size folds the sidebar sooner.
- `sidebar` is put away by Ctrl+Alt+Right past its minimum width or by dragging its edge past it. The title bar's members button brings it back at its last width, Ctrl+Alt+Left does too. It is layout state (`state.json`), not a setting.
- `close-to-tray` decides whether the close button hides the window to the tray or quits. Its row ("Keep Lobby running in the tray when the window closes") is in the Window section only while the app has a tray icon.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Ctrl+, anywhere in the window.
- Click the cog in the title bar (`settings-toggle`).

## Driving it with the scenario harness

Preconditions:

- Layer 3 and 4 clients both have a tray (the driven one's icon is hidden), so Close to tray shows.

- **Open and close.** `{"press": "ctrl-,"}`, wait `/settings/open` = true, `{"press": "escape"}` or `{"click": "settings-back"}`.
- **Look.** What shows is in `/settings/shown` (`theme` is the theme's name, `dark` is always true).
- **Scroll.** In the small window the page scrolls: `{"wheel": "settings-body", "notches": -20}` brings the Window section into view.
- **Rename.** `{"right_click": "member-alice"}`, `{"click": "rename-open"}` (the field has the focus), `{"press": "ctrl-a"}`, `{"type": "new_name"}`, `{"click": "rename-save"}` (or `{"press": "enter"}`). Check `/lobby/member_panel/own/name` (`editing`, `typed`, `can_save`, `saving`, `said`), and on the other client `/lobby/members/new_name/presence` and `/lobby/feed`.
- **Email.** `{"right_click": "member-alice"}` and check `/lobby/member_panel/own/email` (`alice@lobby.test` for `alice`).
- **Kept.** `{"close": "a"}`, `{"open": "a"}`, then check the same paths.
- **Size.** `{"press": "ctrl-="}`, `ctrl--`, `ctrl-0`; or `{"click": "size-larger"}` / `size-smaller`. Check `/settings/ui_scale` (a whole percent) and `/settings/shown/font_size` (16 at 100). 125 is not a step: use 120 or 130.
- **Sidebar.** `ctrl-alt-right` at the minimum, or `{"drag": "sidebar-edge", "by": [100, 0]}`; check `/lobby/sidebar_collapsed` and `/lobby/sidebar_width` (design pixels); `{"click": "members-toggle"}` brings it back.
- Scenarios, all out of the suite and in git history (see the README): `t14b-ui-scale`, `t14b-ui-scale-small` (480x400, 80 to 150), `t14b-collapse-sidebar` and `t14b-live` (all three kept across a restart, both layers), `t14a-settings` (the cog, Close to tray, restart), `t14c-cog` and `t14c-cog-small` (the cog on the URL step, Sign In and over the Lobby, at 480x400 and 150 percent), `t14d-leave` (the owner cannot, dismiss, then leave one of two Lobbies and land in the other, the other client sees the departure) and `t14d-leave-small` (480x400 and 150 percent, leaving the last Lobby ends on the URL step), `t14d-live` (avatar, icon and leave on the real binaries, both layers), `t14d-icon` and `t14d-icon-small` (any Member sets, replaces and removes the icon; the title bar and the menu draw it; at 480x400 and 150 percent), `t14d-avatar` and `t14d-avatar-small` (a picture set, seen by the second client in the roster and on loaded Messages, a refused file, Remove; at 480x400 and 150 percent), `t14c-rename` and `t14c-rename-small` (the Name row: invalid, taken, saved, seen by a second client), `t14a-appearance` (always dark, Size, both layers; it dates from the tabs), `t14a-appearance-small` (480×400).

## Gotchas

- Scenario shots are not byte-stable between runs: a Lobby's mark colour comes from its random port and the feed shows the clock. Only the render tests (`target/ui-shots/lobby_view`, ...) are exact, so a pass that must change nothing compares those.
- `window.rem_size` is what lays everything out, and `appearance::apply` sets it through `Theme.font_size`. The OS window's minimum size cannot change at run time: at 480x400 and 150 percent some content scrolls.
- The DWM window border follows the OS, not the theme.
